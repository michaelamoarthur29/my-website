Overview

The Problem: Misinformation Needs a Trained Response

Science for Georgia is a science communication nonprofit. My internship centered on a workshop concept designed to give medical professionals a trained, practiced response to misinformation — not just facts to recite, but behavioral tools for the actual conversation.

The workshop combines three behavioral science frameworks — the Truth Effect, Identity Protective Cognition, and the Backfire Effect — with improv-based exercises, so participants build muscle memory for de-escalating and redirecting misinformed conversations in real time, rather than just absorbing theory.

Facts alone don't change minds mid-conversation — clinicians need a rehearsed, human way to respond when a patient pushes back.

Process

From Accreditation Research to Outreach

01

CME Accreditation Research

Researched Continuing Medical Education accreditation pathways and identified joint providership as the recommended route for the program — with Morehouse School of Medicine flagged as the strongest institutional partner given mission alignment. A key finding that shaped the whole strategy: communication-skills training like this is exempt from ACCME's financial disclosure requirements, lowering a real barrier to partnership.

02

Business Plan & Research Portfolio

Built a full PRD-style business plan for the program, plus a misinformation research portfolio (Word document with appendices) synthesizing the academic literature behind the Truth Effect, Identity Protective Cognition, and Backfire Effect frameworks — grounding the workshop design in evidence rather than intuition.

03

Personas, Outreach & Public Page

Developed two institutional-buyer proto-personas — Jordan Ellis and Dr. Renee Ortiz — to represent the decision-makers the program actually needs to convince. Wrote outreach emails segmented by institution type (school of medicine vs. membership organization) targeting Morehouse School of Medicine, Emory University, MAG, and NMA, and shipped a public-facing Lovable webpage introducing the program.
